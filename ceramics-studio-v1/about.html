A small room with a very hot box in it.

Kiln started in 2022 in a converted bike workshop, with one wheel, a second-hand kiln and a lot of optimism. Now it's two potters, five wheels and a waiting list for Tuesday nights.

What we believe

Use the good cup.

We don't make pots for shelves. We make them for breakfast, for soup on a Tuesday, for the flowers you picked on the way home. If it chips, it was loved; bring it back and we'll mend it with gold.

Process

From a bag of clay to your table

Every piece takes about three weeks and passes through our hands at least a dozen times.

  1. Wedge & throw

    Clay is kneaded, centred and thrown on the wheel.

  2. Trim

    Leather-hard pots are turned over and a foot is carved.

  3. Bisque fire

    A first, slow firing hardens the clay so it can take glaze.

    1,000°C · 10 hours

  4. Glaze

    Each piece is dipped by hand, and the foot is wiped clean.

  5. Glaze fire

    The second firing melts the glaze into glass.

    1,280°C · 12 hours
