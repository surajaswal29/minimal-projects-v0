Good to know

Ordering & care

How do I order?

This is a demo shop, so there's no checkout. On a real site each piece would link to a payment page; for now, send us a note with the pieces you'd like.

Are the pieces food and dishwasher safe?

Yes. Every glaze is lead-free, fired to 1,280°C and tested for leaching. Dishwashers are fine; the glaze will outlast the machine.

Why does my cup look different from the photo?

Glazes move in the kiln. Where a pot sits, how thick the glaze went on and how fast the kiln cooled all change the colour a little. That's the point.

Do you ship?

Within the UK in three to five days, wrapped in recycled paper and cardboard. No plastic.
