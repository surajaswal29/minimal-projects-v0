Questions

Before you book

What should I wear?

Short nails, sleeves you can roll up and shoes you don't love. We have aprons and towels.

What if I miss a week?

Let us know and you can make it up at an open studio session in the same term.

Can I buy a class as a gift?

Yes, gift vouchers are valid for a year and cover any class. Get in touch and we'll post one out.
