What we believe

Three quiet principles

These guide every decision, from the first sketch to the final pixel.

  1. 01

    Less, but better

    We remove until it hurts, then keep only what earns its place. Fewer features, finished properly.

  2. 02

    Respect attention

    No dark patterns, no endless feeds. Our products measure success by how quickly you can leave.

  3. 03

    Built to last

    Durable materials, maintained codebases and honest pricing. We still support our first product.

How we work

A slow, deliberate process

Small senior teams, short feedback loops, and plenty of time to think. Most engagements run eight to sixteen weeks.

Discuss your product →

  1. Listen

    Interviews, field visits and a close look at what already works. Two weeks.

  2. Shape

    Sketches, prototypes and one clear direction agreed together. Three weeks.

  3. Build

    Design and engineering side by side, shipping weekly to real users.

  4. Care

    Launch is the beginning. We measure, refine and stay on for the long run.

Questions

Frequently asked

Can’t find what you’re looking for? Send us a note.

What kind of projects do you take on?

New products and meaningful redesigns across mobile, web and physical objects. We take on four client projects a year alongside our own products.

How much does an engagement cost?

Most projects range from $60k to $180k depending on scope. We share a fixed proposal after a free discovery call.

Do you work with early-stage startups?

Yes — some of our favourite work is with founding teams. We offer a lighter six-week “foundations” sprint for pre-seed companies.

Where is the team based?

We’re a remote-first team of fourteen with a small studio in Lisbon. We collaborate comfortably across European and US time zones.
